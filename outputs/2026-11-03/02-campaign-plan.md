# NovaBrew Campaign Plan: "Pop the Energy"

## 1. Campaign Overview
* **Campaign Name:** Pop the Energy
* **Big Idea:** Show NovaBrew as the ultimate grab-and-go companion for students and young professionals surviving busy days across Europe.

---

## 2. Key Messages
1. **Instant Convenience:** Grab a can and go—no waiting in line, just great cold coffee ready in seconds.
2. **Aesthetic & Bold:** Stand out with striking deep blue and warm orange visuals that match your style.
3. **Your Daily Fuel:** The perfect companion for lectures, commutes, and the afternoon slump.

---

| Day | Platform | Video idea | Goal |

## 4. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
1. **Follower Growth:** Gain 5,000 new followers across TikTok and Instagram combined in 7 days.
2. **Video Views:** Reach 100,000 total views on TikTok and YouTube Shorts using strong hooks.
3. **Engagement Rate:** Achieve a 5% engagement rate (likes, comments, and shares) on all Instagram Reels.
