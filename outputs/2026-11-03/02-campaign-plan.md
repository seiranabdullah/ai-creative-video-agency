# NovaBrew Launch Campaign Plan

## 1. Campaign Name & Big Idea
* **Campaign Name:** Pop the Blue, Feel the Orange.
* **Big Idea:** Show how a can of NovaBrew instantly upgrades your study or work routine with great taste and zero hassle.

## 2. Key Messages
* Grab and go: The ultimate ready-to-drink coffee for busy days.
* Bold flavor: Smooth, cold coffee that always hits the spot.
* Stand out: Match your vibe with our deep blue and warm orange aesthetic.
